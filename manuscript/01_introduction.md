{{PACKAGE_NAME}} is the one surface an operator talks to. Its role, `{{CODENAME}}`, accepts a verb and a target: `brief`/`recon`/`plan`/`execute`/`debrief` a single film, `run` a canned multi-package mission through the orchestrator, `list` the fleet from the coordinator registry, `status` the suite snapshot, and `gadgets` inspect a film's registered gadget payloads.
